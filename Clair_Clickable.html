Clair
Clair is an open-source, API-driven vulnerability scanner most commonly paired with a registry platform like Quay — it scans images as they land in a registry it's connected to, rather than as a step in a CI pipeline the way Trivy typically works. Its architecture means scanning happens automatically once set up, but that automation only covers the registries it's actually been told about.
